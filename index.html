Legal & operating detail
  • Home-based RSGE is a different regime from a private daycare centre permit.
  • Provider alone: up to 6 children, subject to recognition and approved capacity.
  • 7–9 children requires an assistant and BC approval for that capacity.
  • Home requirements include appropriate rooms, safety equipment, first aid, evacuation procedures and suitable child equipment.
  • Recognized operation can be subsidized or non-subsidized; the current private planning rate is $70/day.
