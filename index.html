Keep the two legal regimes separate

Home-based RSGE

This model

  • Self-employed individual.
  • Recognized through the Bureau coordonnateur.
  • Up to 6 children alone; up to 9 with an assistant, subject to legal and BC approval.
  • Can be subsidized or non-subsidized.

Private daycare centre

Different path

  • Ministère permit.
  • Commercial-centre rules.
  • Different staffing, floor-space and fees.
  • Do not import those costs here.

Source guardrail

  • Act: capacity and operating structure.
  • Regulation: recognition, training and safety.
  • BC: local process and practical approval.

Expandable details

Recognition & documents
  • Open the file with the Bureau coordonnateur.
  • Prepare identity documents, CV, health declaration, first aid, liability insurance and evacuation procedure.
  • Complete police / absence-of-impediment checks.
  • Complete required food-hygiene training.
Capacity: 1–6 versus 7–9
  • Provider alone: up to 6 children, subject to recognition limits.
  • 7–9 children requires an assistant.
  • Confirm the exact approved capacity for this specific lower apartment with the BC.
  • Do not assume the home qualifies for 9 simply because the statute allows that ceiling.
Home compliance
  • Kitchen, eating area, sanitary facilities and a children's games/activity room with an exterior window.
  • Smoke and carbon-monoxide detection, extinguisher, first-aid kit and suitable equipment.
  • Appropriate sleep equipment based on child ages.
  • Emergency evacuation procedure and drills.
Funding & payment mechanics
  • Model recognized + non-subsidized separately from recognized + subsidized.
  • Private path: provider sets the parent rate; current planning assumption is $70/day.
  • Subsidized path: confirm current parent contribution, government allocation and supplements with the BC.
  • Ask for a sample payment statement showing gross, deductions and net.
Costs still to research
  • First aid, MAPAQ, checks and insurance.
  • Food, supplies, equipment and cleaning.
  • Training after recognition.
  • Holiday, sick-day and closure effects.
  • Taxes and self-employment reserves.
