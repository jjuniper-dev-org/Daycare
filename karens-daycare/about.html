About Karen's Home Daycare

Karen's Home Daycare is a small, family-style daycare in Chelsea. The goal is simple: a safe, caring place where children feel comfortable, and where parents feel confident leaving them.

Our approach

Safe and caring

A child-friendly home where each child is looked after like family.

Consistent routines

Predictable days help children feel secure.

Open communication

Parents are kept in the loop about their child's day.
